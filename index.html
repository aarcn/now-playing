Swipes can't tell which arm you used, but taps can. A tap on the R1 ring always plays or pauses, and play/pause is also in the menu.

Some covers couldn't be downloaded, so those songs use the text-only screen.

LyricsLines that follow along with the song, from LRCLIB, a free community lyrics database. Not every song has them. Also in the glasses menu.
